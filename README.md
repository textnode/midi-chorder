Preparation: 

In the script, update the dictionary "channel_and_note_mapping" to map your control pads to the degrees of the major scale.

(If you don't know these ahead of time, try running the script and press the pads while examining the console output - it will show you the channel and note for each pad press and release).

Usage:

Run the script

Press and hold your desired combination of control pads and while holding them press the tonic of the chord you want on the midi keyboard keys.
